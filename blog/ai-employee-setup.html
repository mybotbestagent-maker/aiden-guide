What Is Aiden, Exactly?

Aiden is a personal AI assistant built on top of OpenClaw — a platform that lets you deploy a persistent, memory-equipped AI agent that actually runs on your own server or machine.

Unlike ChatGPT (which is a great conversation tool but forgets you every session), Aiden has persistent memory. It knows your business, your clients, your tone of voice, your processes. You teach it once — it remembers forever.

Think of it as the difference between hiring a temp worker every day versus having a loyal team member who knows the job inside out.

The 3-Step Setup

1

Install OpenClaw and Deploy Aiden

OpenClaw runs on any Linux server or even a $10/month VPS. The Aiden Guide walks you through the full installation — it takes about 20 minutes. You get a live AI agent running in your Telegram (or any other channel you use).

2

Train It On Your Business

This is the magic part. You tell Aiden about your business: what you do, how you talk to clients, what questions you get most often, what your pricing looks like. You write this in plain English — no coding, no technical knowledge needed. Aiden reads it and remembers it permanently.

3

Connect Your Channels

Connect Aiden to Telegram, email, or your website chat. From that moment on, it monitors incoming messages and responds — or at minimum drafts responses for your quick approval. You choose how autonomous it is.
